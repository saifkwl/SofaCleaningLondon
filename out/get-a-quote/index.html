Questions

Quotes and booking

Is the estimator price what I will actually pay?

It is an estimate built from our "from" prices, so treat it as the lower end of a range. Fabric, soil level and access all move the real figure, which is why the tool shows a range rather than a single number. You get one exact price at the walk-through, before any equipment comes off the van.

What happens after I send the WhatsApp message?

We read it, and if anything is unclear we ask — usually about the fabric, or whether a mark is a spill or a wear patch. Then we come back with a firm price and the dates we have available. There is no obligation at any point, and no call-out fee for quoting.

Can I send photos instead of filling this in?

Yes, and photos are genuinely useful. A picture of the sofa, a close-up of any bad marks and a shot of the care label under a cushion tells us more than a form does. Send them straight to our WhatsApp number and skip the estimator entirely.

Do I have to book once I have a price?

No. Quoting is free and carries no commitment. If our price is higher than you expected, or you decide the sofa is not worth cleaning, that is the end of it — we would rather tell you a piece is past saving than take payment for a clean that will disappoint you.

How quickly can you come?

Usually within two to three working days, and often the same week. If your date is fixed — a tenancy check-out, a move, guests arriving — say so in the message and we will tell you straight away whether we can hit it.
